# V5.2.0 RC6 - inventory of new controls (for your future Quick Preset assignments)

All default to **Native / Auto** (option 0). None is assigned by any Quick Preset yet.

When you decide preset values, give them as option numbers from the "Options" column. They are then added to:
- the XML preset entries;
- the script preset function.

Editing such a row will switch Quick Preset to Off, like every other preset row.

| Row (menu) | Key | Engine variable (native) | Options (1, 2, ...) | Renderer | When it applies | Saved by game? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PT: Scene Range | `PTSceneRange` (core group) | ProximityTLASSize + ProximityExtendedTLASSize (400 / 800 m) | 300 m, 250 m, 200 m, 150 m (only ever smaller) | PT | immediately | no |
| PT: Skin Bounce Light Desaturation | `URTX_PTSkinBounceDesat` | PTSkinIndirectDesaturation (0.5) | 0, 0.25, 0.75, 1 | PT | immediately | no |
| PT: Ray-Traced Caustics | `URTX_PTCaustics` | CausticsEnable (Off) | On, Off | PT | immediately | no |
| PT: Caustics Light the Scene | `URTX_PTCausticsInPT` | CausticsOutputToPT (Off) | On, Off | PT | immediately | no |
| PT: Caustics Types | `URTX_PTCausticsTypes` | CausticsMode (all three = 7) | Through water only (1), On the water surface only (2), Reflected only (4) | PT | immediately | no |
| PT: Caustics Resolution | `URTX_PTCausticsRes` | CausticsCasterResolution (2048) | 512, 1024, 4096 | PT | immediately | no |
| PT: Caustics Blur | `URTX_PTCausticsBlur` | CausticsResolveRadius (2) | 0, 1, 4, 8 | PT | immediately | no |
| PT: Caustics Area Around the Camera | `URTX_PTCausticsArea` | CausticsHalfExtent (60 m) | 20, 40, 100, 150, 200 m | PT | immediately | no |
| RT/PT: Fog in Ray-Traced Reflections | `URTX_RTReflFog` | RtRadianceShadingFog (On) | On, Off | Normal RT + PT | immediately | no |
| Terrain Preload Distance | `URTX_TerrainPreload` | TerrainReadAheadDistance (20 m) | 10, 40, 100, 200, 400, 600 m | all | newly loaded areas | yes: session-only protection (no native-menu overlap: verified) |
| Tree and Bush Draw Distance *(not shipped)* | `URTX_TreeDistance` | FoliageDistanceScale (1.0) | 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.6, 3, 4, 5 | all | newly loaded areas | native Foliage Visibility Range setting: not shipped |
